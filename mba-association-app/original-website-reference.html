Association

More than a club. A professional ecosystem.

The Association exists to give MBA students a structured path from classroom concepts to real responsibility — through four connected stages that build on one another across the academic year.

01

Learn

Gain practical knowledge beyond classrooms, drawn directly from your club's discipline.

02

Participate

Get opportunities to contribute, engage in projects and represent your club.

03

Lead

Take responsibility for outcomes and develop genuine leadership habits.

04

Grow

Build the professional confidence and capability that classroom work alone can't give.

Inside the Community

Selection Process

How office bearers are chosen.

Each club selects five office bearers — one President, one Vice President and three Committee Members — through the same five-step process, for fifteen office bearers in total. The Chairman and Vice Chairman are then elected from among these fifteen.

Rules of Conduct

What every member commits to.

Governance

A student-led platform operating under the guidance and authority of the MBA Department / College.

The MBA Department / College retains final authority over the Association and its activities. Every decision made within the Association operates within that framework.

Coordination

Transparency

Responsibility

Handover

One team. One academic year. A legacy to continue.

Office bearers normally serve for one academic year, unless otherwise decided by the college or department. Outgoing teams hand over relevant records, financial information, activity documentation, ongoing work and organisational knowledge to the team that follows them.
